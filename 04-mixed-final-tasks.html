Спроба 1 · Energy sources and prices

The pie chart below shows the sources of electricity in one country in 2024, while the table shows the average monthly electricity bill in four regions in 2018 and 2024. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant. Write at least 150 words.

ІнструкціяТри кроки: introduction → overview → body. Спочатку читай англійську підказку, потім пиши. Після трьох частин відкриється повна модель.

Крок 1 · Introduction

HintOne line, two charts: «The pie chart shows X, while the table compares Y.» Include the years.

Sample: The pie chart shows the sources of electricity in one country in 2024, while the table compares average monthly electricity bills in four regions in 2018 and 2024.

Крок 2 · Overview

HintOne sentence per chart: pie (renewables dominate) + table (bills rose everywhere; South highest). Optional honest link: «a shift that mirrors the changing energy mix» — only if you believe it.

Sample: Overall, renewables dominate the country's electricity supply, and household bills rose in every region over the six-year period, with the South consistently the most expensive.

Крок 3 · Body 1 + Body 2

Hint · groupingBody 1 = pie only: renewables over half (well over half), gas, coal — fractions + verbs of share. Body 2 = table only: all four regions rose; South highest in both years (+39); differences modest. Never mix pie phrases into the table paragraph.

The South remained the most expensive throughout, rising from £95 to £134, while the North saw a similar increase, of £36. §1 The pie chart shows the sources of electricity in one country in 2024, while the table compares average monthly electricity bills in four regions in 2018 and 2024.

§2 Overall, renewables dominate the country's electricity supply, and household bills rose in every region over the six-year period, with the South consistently the most expensive.

Порівняй рішення: чи чисті абзаци (pie-фрази тільки в §3)? Чи «over half / almost a third / just 18%» замість сухих відсотків?

Спроба 2 · Cinema and streaming

The bar chart below shows cinema visits per person per year by age group in 2024, while the line graph shows the percentage of households with a streaming subscription between 2015 and 2025. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant. Write at least 150 words.

Єдина підказка · groupingBody 1 = bar only: 16-24 lead (6.2 visits), declining with age; one multiplier (nearly three times the 55+ figure). Body 2 = line only: subscriptions rose from 18% to 83%; optional honest link at the END of §4 («the two trends point in opposite directions»).
ІнструкціяПиши всю відповідь одразу (§1–§4, 150+ слів). Без таймера. Після чек-листа відкриється модель.
0 слів (мінімум 150)

Чек-лист перед моделлю

§1 The bar chart shows cinema visits per person per year by age group in 2024, while the line graph shows the percentage of households with a streaming subscription from 2015 to 2025.

§2 Overall, cinema attendance is highest among young people and falls steadily with age, while streaming subscriptions rose dramatically over the decade. The two trends appear to point in opposite directions, although the charts do not prove a direct link.

§3 According to the bar chart, 16-24 year olds visit the cinema most often, at 6.2 visits per person per year — nearly three times the figure for the over-55s, at just 2.2. Attendance declines steadily across the age groups, with the 25-39 and 40-54 groups recording 4.1 and 3.0 visits respectively.

§4 Meanwhile, streaming subscriptions climbed from 18% of households in 2015 to 83% in 2025. Growth was particularly rapid in the second half of the period, with subscriptions more than doubling between 2019 and 2025. By the end of the period, streaming had become a mainstream service in the majority of households.

Порівняй рішення: чи «nearly three times the figure for the over-55s»? Чи чесний зв'язок у кінці §2 з застереженням?

Спроба 3 · Libraries — table + pie chart · exam conditions

The table below shows the number of library visitors in four UK cities in 2018 and 2024, while the pie chart shows the main reasons for visiting the library in 2024. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant. Write at least 150 words.

Інструкція · режим іспиту3 хв — аналіз: один тренд таблиці (усі виросли, Оксфорд лідер), лідер pie (books), друга причина (study). 15 хв — письмо. 2 хв — перевірка: обидва графіки в overview? абзаци чисті? жодного вигаданого зв'язку? Після чек-листа — модель і перепис.
20:00
0 слів (мінімум 150)

Чек-лист перед моделлю (усі 8 — чесно)

§1 The table shows the number of library visitors in four UK cities in 2018 and 2024, while the pie chart shows the main reasons for visiting the library in 2024.

§2 Overall, visitor numbers grew in every city over the period, with Oxford consistently the busiest, and borrowing books remains the primary reason for visiting, followed by study and work.

§3 According to the table, Oxford recorded the highest visitor numbers in both years, rising from 500,000 to 590,000. York and Bath also saw healthy growth, of 110,000 and 70,000 visitors respectively, while Exeter remained the smallest library service despite an increase of 70,000.

§4 Turning to the pie chart, borrowing books is the main motivation, cited by 42% of visitors. Study and work account for almost a third, while internet access and events attract only 15% and 12% respectively.

Порівняй рішення: «respectively» у правильному порядку? «cited by / account for / attract» — три різні дієслова частки?
